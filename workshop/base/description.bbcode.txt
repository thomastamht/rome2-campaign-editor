[h1]Total War: Rome 2 Web Editor[/h1]
Edit your running campaign from a web page.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] View a live campaign map
[*] Create armies
[*] Add money to the treasury
[*] Force war, peace, trade or vassalage
[*] Repair buildings
[*] Change region owner
[*] Change public order
[*] Add population surplus
[*] Trigger rebellions
[*] Add experience to generals, agents and units
[*] Change action points
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Press Windows+R and run: [code]powershell.exe mkdir $env:USERPROFILE\rome2_web_editor_mod[/code]
[*] Enable the mod and load a campaign.
[*] Open [url=https://thomastamht.github.io/rome2-campaign-editor/]the web page[/url] in Chrome or Edge, click Connect folder, and choose the folder from step 1.
[/olist]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Tested on Windows only.
Can be added mid-save.
Conflicts only with mods that replace lua_scripts/episodicscripting.lua.
For DEI features, also subscribe to the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827602931]DEI patch[/url].

[h2]Credits[/h2]
Vibe coded with AI. Based on IMRZ's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2295560801]Web campaign editor[/url] for Warhammer II.
Questions or bugs? Leave a comment.
