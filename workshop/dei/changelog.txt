0.2.0
- The patch now carries the DEI features and no longer replaces any game script; any load order
- In a DEI campaign the unit list shows only DEI units, and regions' population (nobles, citizens, poor, foreigners) can be edited
- Without this patch the editor stays vanilla, even in DEI

- DEI 战役里兵种列表只显示 DEI 兵种，可修改地区人口（贵族、公民、贫民、外邦人）
- 不装此补丁时，编辑器在 DEI 里也只有原版功能
