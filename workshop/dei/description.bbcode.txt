[h1]Total War: Rome 2 Web Editor — DEI patch[/h1]
DEI features for the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827602956]web editor[/url].

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Show only DEI units
[*] Edit region population: nobles, citizens, poor, foreigners
[/list]

[h2]Install[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Press Windows+R and run: [code]powershell.exe mkdir $env:USERPROFILE\rome2_web_editor_mod[/code]
[*] Enable this patch and the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827602956]web editor[/url], then load a Divide et Impera campaign.
[*] Open [url=https://thomastamht.github.io/rome2-campaign-editor/]the web page[/url] in Chrome or Edge, click Connect folder, and choose the folder from step 1.
[/olist]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Tested on Windows only.
Requires Divide et Impera.
Can be added mid-save.
Any load order. Replaces no game file.

[h2]Credits[/h2]
Vibe coded with AI. Based on IMRZ's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2295560801]Web campaign editor[/url] for Warhammer II.
Questions or bugs? Leave a comment.

[h1]全面战争：罗马2 网页编辑器 — DEI 补丁[/h1]

[list]
[*] 兵种列表只显示 DEI 兵种
[*] 修改地区人口：贵族、公民、贫民、外邦人
[/list]

[h2]安装[/h2]
[olist]
[*] 按 Windows+R，运行：[code]powershell.exe mkdir $env:USERPROFILE\rome2_web_editor_mod[/code]
[*] 启用本补丁和[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3827602956]网页编辑器[/url]，载入 DEI 战役。
[*] 用 Chrome 或 Edge 打开[url=https://thomastamht.github.io/rome2-campaign-editor/]网页[/url]，点「连接文件夹」，选择第 1 步的文件夹。
[/olist]

只在 Windows 上测试过。
需要 Divide et Impera。
可中途加入存档。
